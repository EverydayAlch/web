# Tim's story (true account, in his words)

Status: not on the site. The live story section in `index.html` (`#story`) keeps the old copy. This is stored because it is true.
Ends at the low point; Tim stopped it at "I've done everything. Everything." The later paragraphs (meditation, waiting on Source, embodiment) were cut.

---

I'd been an alchemist for years. So much transformation in my life. I was in such a better place. I didn't spend my days stewing on things that happened to me. I knew I was going to be okay in all aspects.

And yet my life wasn't in shambles. I had one contract I was living off of. $3,000 a month — not enough by any means. I was hanging out with a beautiful woman, and I could pay for my life, kinda. And I had this awesome human being in it. I thought, "I'm going in the right direction. Things are starting to go my way."

And then, right before I was supposed to go visit her, the contract went away. My business went bankrupt. My bank account dropped to negative. I had to move out of the house I was staying in. I moved back in with my family. I couldn't go see her. She decided that was not going to work for her.

And in one fell swoop, I found myself alone in the basement of my parents' house, with no lover, no revenue, no income, no money in my bank account, no investments. And all I could do was look at the work and think, *what the fuck is going on here?* I've done everything. Everything.
